# Never More Miserable: notes

id: 07-never-more-miserable
kind: original, category mental-health
Rhetorical model: FDR first inaugural (March 4, 1933) and Lincoln's "House Divided" (Springfield, June 16, 1858).

Word count: 16,475 (wc -w speech.md, including headings and cues)
Estimated runtime at 140 wpm: about 118 minutes (1 h 58 min)

## How the models are used

- Lincoln's opening premise (first know where we are and whither we are tending) frames sections I-VIII as an assessment before any prescription.
- Lincoln's "house divided" image becomes the thesis: a country cannot stand permanently half prosperous and half despairing, and the rich/poor depression gap is the literal dividing wall (section XI).
- Lincoln's "timbers" device (separately cut pieces that fit one frame) is adapted in section X, with one deliberate change: the speech says explicitly that no architect or plot is behind our timbers. The fit comes from many uncoordinated choices. This keeps the device and avoids any conspiracy framing.
- FDR's structure: candor up front, catalogue of the damage, insistence that the problem is not scarcity (our granaries are full), indictment of a false measure of success (FDR's money changers become our habit of measuring a nation only by output), the moral claim that happiness is not in the mere possession of money, then a concrete call to action and a close in unity.
- FDR's famous fear line is quoted once, attributed. The speech's answer: the thing to fear is not the numbers but the shrug.
- Techniques: anaphora ("Look at...", "We have..."), tricolon, antithesis (richer/sadder), direct address, four "witnesses" device, concrete composite sketches clearly presented as illustrations, not real people.

## Honesty notes (kept in the speech)

- "Never" is defended as "never in the decades our instruments have been running": Gallup since 2015, NHANES PHQ-9 since 2005ish (brief compares to 2013-2014), GSS since 1972, WHR since 2012. The speech says plainly we have no comparable survey for 1933 or 1863.
- Rising diagnosis partly reflects less stigma and more screening; the speech concedes this and answers with measures that don't depend on diagnosis (GSS happiness, WHR life evaluations, PHQ-9 symptom screens in a random sample, suicide rates).
- GSS 2021 was a push-to-web survey during COVID; self-administered modes tend to read gloomier. Conceded in the speech.
- Counter-evidence acknowledged: overdose deaths fell from 105,007 (2023) to 79,384 (2024); life expectancy rose to 79.0 in 2024; YRBS sadness edged down from 2021 to 2023; Gallup Q1 2026 (19.1%) slightly below Q4 2025 peak (20.0%); US WHR rank rose one place in 2026.
- Social media causation is presented as contested (Haidt vs. Odgers; WHR 2026 itself says the picture is complex).
- Composite illustrations ("a woman in Ohio", etc.) are explicitly framed as illustrations, not real people.
- Policy proposals in section XIII are framed as the speaker's opinion and are not attributed to any study.
