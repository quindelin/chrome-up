## Safe-messaging choices

- No description of any suicide method. Firearms are mentioned once, only as a
  share of deaths and in the context of means safety (time and distance in a
  crisis), as suicide-prevention organizations recommend.
- Suicide is presented as preventable, not inevitable or understandable as a
  "solution"; recovery and help-seeking are named; 988 (call, text, or chat)
  is given twice, including at the close.
- No individual cases are described; the only "stories" are explicitly
  composites or hypotheticals, labelled as such in the speech.

## Stated as general knowledge (not separately fetched)

- Most American adults lack a four-year degree (Census: roughly 38 percent of
  adults 25 and over hold one).
- Suicide is a leading cause of death among teenagers and young adults; asking
  directly about suicide does not plant the idea; suicidal crises are often
  brief (standard suicide-prevention guidance from 988, AFSP, SPRC).
- High suicide rates in American Indian and Alaska Native communities.
- PNTR with China (2000) and China's WTO entry (2001).
- Amos 8:11 allusion ("famine ... of hearing the words"), public domain.
- SHADAC is based at the University of Minnesota.
