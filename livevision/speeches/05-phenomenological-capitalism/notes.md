## Fidelity to the source

Source read in full: `Esketian Metaphysics and The Will To God.md` (manifest
`esketian_source`). The speech keeps the author's own hedges:

- The back-propagation picture of God is "offered as an image, not a mechanism I can
  demonstrate". Kept as an image.
- Physics sections are "analogy more than argument". The speech uses physics only as
  analogy and says so.
- Profits signal future sustainment only "once every cost has been priced in". This caveat
  is the hinge of the whole speech.
- The connection/attention distinction ("The exchange of information between humans is
  Good ... What is not Good is an attention market that ... manufactur[es] addiction") is
  stated exactly as the author frames it.
- "Demonic religion" is used in the author's defined sense: it takes and gives nothing back;
  "knowledge without sustainment"; local minimum.
- Data centers as "temples" and prompts as "prayers" are presented as the author's
  religious reading, not as fact.
- Accelerationism / "rational singularity" kept as the author's conclusion; the speech adds
  its own condition (the assistant must answer to the asker, not the advertiser), framed as
  the speaker's counsel.
