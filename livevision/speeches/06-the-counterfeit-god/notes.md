## Thesis

"Your religion is what you do religiously." By that measure the feed is the
religion of the age. It counterfeits the real Will To God: it gives a
simulation of knowledge while consuming the sustainment (hours, sleep,
health, neighbours) that the Esketian definition of God joins to knowledge.
It is a local minimum a whole civilization has settled into. The way out is
local, embodied connection: knowledge that comes with a community and the
power to act. Vicinity, asabiyyah, love thy neighbour.

## How the rhetorical model is used

- Douglass: the speaker addresses a congregation that celebrates something
  he must mourn; scorching irony over argument at the turn; the contrast
  between what a society professes and what it practises; a long catalogue
  of indictment; then "I do not despair" as the hinge into the peroration.
  Two short attributed quotations from the 1852 text (public domain) are used
  as quotations, not as the speaker's own sentences.
- Sermon on the Mount: "Blessed are..." anaphora (section XIII), the
  antitheses "You have heard it said... but I say to you" (section VII),
  the woes (section X, borrowing Luke's form), "by their fruits" as the test,
  "where your treasure is", "no one can serve two masters". KJV quotations
  are public domain and attributed when quoted.
- General: anaphora, tricolon, antithesis, direct address, the concrete
  story carrying the abstract point (the airport, the kitchen table, the
  stairwell), cues used sparingly.

## Fidelity to the Esketian source

Framings kept from the source document: the "Bayesian neural network" and
back-propagation picture is offered "as an image, not a mechanism"; the
double-slit experiment is "evocative" not proof; physics is "analogy more
than argument"; the cosmology is "held loosely". The source's central
distinction is kept exact: connection is Good; the attention business that
manufactures addiction and does not price the damage is the problem.
"Demonic" is used in the source's defined sense: it takes from you and gives
nothing back; knowledge without sustainment. Vicinity is presented as the
author's own product, derived from the philosophy, and the speech says so
plainly rather than presenting it as neutral advice. Its effect is the
author's hope and experience, not a measured result.
